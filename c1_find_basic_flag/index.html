Welcome to San Francisco!

San Francisco is a vibrant metropolis on the West Coast of the United States. Known for its steep hills, the iconic Golden Gate Bridge, and liberal culture, the city attracts visitors from around the world.

Top Attractions

🌉 Golden Gate Bridge

The iconic landmark of San Francisco. The bridge is 1.7 miles long and opened in 1937.

🏝️ Alcatraz

The famous prison island, now a museum and one of the most visited sites in the city.

🚃 Cable Cars

The historic streetcars are a must-ride for every visitor to San Francisco.

Culture & Lifestyle

🍽️ Food Scene

Famous for sourdough bread, fresh seafood, and a diverse international cuisine scene.

🎨 Art & Music

Numerous museums, galleries, and the legendary Summer of Love heritage.

🌈 LGBTQ+ Culture

The Castro District is one of the first gay neighborhoods in the world.
