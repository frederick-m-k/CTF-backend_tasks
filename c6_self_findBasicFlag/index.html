Welcome to Tokyo!

Tokyo, Japan's bustling capital, seamlessly blends ultramodern skyscrapers with traditional temples. With over 37 million people in the metropolitan area, it's the world's most populous metropolis, known for its cutting-edge technology, incredible food scene, and vibrant pop culture.

Top Attractions

🚦 Shibuya Crossing

The world's busiest pedestrian crossing, where thousands of people cross at once in a beautiful chaos.

🏯 Senso-ji Temple

Tokyo's oldest temple, dating back to 645 AD, located in the historic Asakusa district.

🗼 Tokyo Tower

An iconic communications and observation tower inspired by the Eiffel Tower.

Culture & Lifestyle

🍣 World-Class Cuisine

Tokyo has more Michelin-starred restaurants than any other city in the world - over 200!

🎮 Pop Culture Capital

Home to Akihabara (electric town), anime culture, video games, and Harajuku street fashion.

🌸 Seasonal Beauty

Famous for cherry blossom season (sakura) in spring and stunning autumn colors.
